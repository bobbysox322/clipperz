Meet the Staff

Aurora Ozzimo

Owner & Founder

Licensed cosmetologist since 1982 — 40+ years behind the chair.

Aurora has honed her craft for over four decades, apprenticing with master barbers and working 13 years at Asheville icons Fox Barbershop and Joe Kings. In 2021 she founded Clipperz, blending her wealth of experience with a warm, artistic touch.

Linda Spears

Stylist · Nails

In the beauty industry since 1982; 13 years at Joe Kings.

Linda moved to Asheville from Springfield, Missouri in 1994. She spent 13 years at Joe Kings Hair Shop serving a wide range of customers — and she handles Clipperz's nail services by appointment.

Kelly Thornton

Stylist

Cutting since 1988 — Supercuts management to classic barbershops.

Kelly started in Indianapolis in 1988, opened and managed a Supercuts in Naples, FL, then honed her barbering in local shops. She moved to Western North Carolina in 2010 and now brings her passion for the perfect haircut to Clipperz.

Deborah Stewart

Stylist

Cutting since 1996 — 20 years co-owning Fox Barbershop.

A Madison County native, Deborah co-owned and operated Fox Barbershop in Asheville with her husband for 20 years. She loves taking her time and building relationships with new clients.
